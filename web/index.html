How this app works

This app manages time studies stored on a connected ESP32 device. Connect the ESP32 over USB and the app talks to it automatically.

  1. Create Study — choose a Job Traveler PDF. The app reads its steps, then you enter the part number and work order number and upload the study to the ESP32 as a CSV file.
  2. Current Studies — browse the CSV studies stored on the device, preview any of them, download a copy, or delete one.
  3. Export — select one or more studies and save them into a single Excel workbook, one worksheet per study.

Each study is saved on the ESP32 using the naming convention <part number>_<work order number>.csv.
